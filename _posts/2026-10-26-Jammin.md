## Description of the Assignment
The purpose of this assignment was to program an Arduino buzzer to play a song transcribed from sheet music, using note frequency constants and arrays for the pitches and durations.

## Where I found the music
I wanted to do the theme song from my favorite show Amphibia. I found the music based off of sheet music online. Then I transcribed all of the notes and counted out all of the rhythms of the notes. Then I had to figure out how to make the melody play, by using the extra resources provided.
