## Description of the Assignment
The purpose of this assignment was to learn how to apply the basics of circuitry using homemade elements.
To close the circuit in two spots we used paper clips and metal snaps.
We prototyped our designs on paper to represent the conductive thread.
Once the prototyped design was confirmed, the circuitry was attached and embroidery embelishments were added to the braclet.


## Tip/Trick for next time
Try to use as little conductive thread as possible when sewing.
