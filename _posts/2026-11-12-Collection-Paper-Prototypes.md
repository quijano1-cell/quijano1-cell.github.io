# The Avatar Cycle

A set of three Avatar: The Last Airbender inspired e-textile pieces that bring the four elements into everyday objects: an Aang plushie, an Appa pillow, and a Toph sweatshirt. 
Using felt, embroidery, and conductive thread, each piece lights up, buzzes, or responds to touch, light, or temperature, blending the show’s characters with interactive textile design.
A set of three Avatar themed e-textile pieces, each tied to one of the characters or elements.

### Description of Aang Plushie
Aang plushie: A standalone felt plushie with its own battery and switch. When turned on, two blue LEDs light up, like his glowing arrows.

![Appa Pillow](https://quijano1-cell.github.io/assets/img/Aang Plushie.JPG)

### Description of Appa Pillow
Appa pillow: A pillow with four element LEDs in the corners (water, earth, fire, air). 
A homemade conductive fabric switch makes two of the LEDs fade while the other two do their own pattern. 
Embroidered element symbols hide the conductive thread traces.
![Toph Sweatshirt](https://quijano1-cell.github.io/assets/img/Appa Pillow.JPG)

### Description of Toph Sweatshirt
Toph sweatshirt: A green sweatshirt with a temperature sensor, a light sensor, a buzzer, and a green LED. 
The buzzer reacts to the temperature while the LED brightness follows the light level, both at once.
