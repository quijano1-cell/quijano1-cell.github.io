# Circuit Plushy
---

## Description of the Assignment
The purpose of this assignment was to learn how to apply the basics of circuitry and the basics of embroidery into a project. We prototyped our designs on paper to represent the conductive thread.
Once the prototyped design was confirmed, we began to sew the plushy using conductive threads and elements of sewing.


## Tip/Trick for next time
Tack the thread of the lights on the side so it doesn't affect the conductive thread when sewing.
